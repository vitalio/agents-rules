- Task & session completion summary: When completing a task or ending a
  session, wait until all changes, final verification checks, and version
  control publishing have completed cleanly in preceding steps. Then in the
  final reply: (1) give a concise, substantive summary in chat covering the key

  uncommitted or unpushed work-tree changes; and (3) record durable
  architectural or operational decisions in project documentation.
